The approach

Write it down.

The engineering posts include an analysis of technology skills and developer salaries using the Stack Overflow Developer Survey. Language learning posts cover vocabulary frequency and the Italian flashcard project.

A trip report from Marmolada brings the recorded track, photographs and video together with an interactive 3D terrain map. Photo times are matched to the route recording to place them along the climb.

A small Python script compiles Markdown into HTML. The site has topic pages, related articles, RSS and a sitemap, with draft posts available through direct links.
